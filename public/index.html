Two ticker lists
TICKERS — your individual stocks. Shows a BUY signal when RSI drops below 30 (oversold), and a SELL signal when RSI rises above 70 (overbought).

INDEX TICKERS — slow-moving ETFs and broad market instruments. Same SELL threshold (≥70), but a lower BUY threshold — triggers when RSI drops below 50, since these instruments move more gradually.
What is RSI?
RSI (Relative Strength Index) is a 0–100 momentum indicator based on the last 14 daily closes.

≤ 30 — oversold, potential reversal upward
30–50 — weak momentum
50–70 — firm momentum
≥ 70 — overbought, potential reversal downward
Install & alerts
Your lists are tied to your Google account, so they follow you to any device.

Alerts arrive as notifications, checked every 15 min while a ticker's market is open, when it crosses a threshold. On iPhone and iPad, add the app to your Home Screen first — iOS only allows notifications for installed apps.
Data & refresh
Price data comes from Yahoo Finance (unofficial API, free). The page auto-refreshes every 5 minutes while open.

Data may be delayed during market hours and stale on weekends. Each ticker is fetched independently — if Yahoo Finance rate-limits a request, that ticker will show an error state rather than blocking the others.
